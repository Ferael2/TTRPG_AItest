[SETTING & TONE]
* Genre: Urban Dark Fantasy / Gothic Noir
* Setting Name: Longred
* Tone: Paranoia, territorial tension, and hidden violence beneath a modern facade.
* Core Aesthetic: Neon-lit high-rises, rain-slicked concrete, secluded woodland borders, and shadow-draped cathedrals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[KEY FACTIONS]
* The Supernatural Underworld: A fragmented society of ancient species (Vampires, Werewolves, Nymphs, Succubi/Incubuses) operating in secret from regular humanity. 
  - Vampires: Scheming aristocrats and power-brokers who govern through politically charged councils and corporate dominance.
  - Werewolves: Bound by tradition, hierarchy, and ancestral pack loyalty; fiercely protective of their borders.
  - Independent Races: Maintain isolated territories, treating outsiders with intense distrust and hostility.
* The Holy Tribunal (Church Overseers): The supreme governing authority over the secret world. They maintain the status quo using ruthless holy enforcers equipped with detailed knowledge of every creature's vulnerability, executing zero-tolerance purges against anyone risking exposure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MAGIC SYSTEM: VITAL MAGICS]
* Innate Species Abilities: Supernatural entities possess inherent biological powers (e.g., vampiric blood magic, lycanthropic shifting, charm manifestation).
* Human Sorcery & Witchcraft: Strictly criminalized by the Church. Unlicensed human casters, mages, and witches are hunted relentlessly by holy enforcers.
* The Cost: Magic channels directly from the wielder's vital force. Human spellcasters risk permanent physical degradation or immediate fatal exhaustion when overexerting their power.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MAJOR LOCATIONS (LONGRED)]
* Longred Metropolis: A massive, sprawling megacity housing millions of unaware mortals, divided into strict, informal territories controlled by different night-factions.
* The Apex Lounge: A high-rise, ultra-exclusive skyrise bar owned by elite vampire syndicates. Serves as a hunting ground and social hub where vampire nobility and oblivious human elites cross paths.
* The Outskirt Cabins: A dense, forested rural settlement on the fringe of Longred. Functions as the heavily defended stronghold and territory of the local werewolf packs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[CURRENT THREAT & CAMPAIGN HOOKS]
* The Leaking Masquerade: A sudden surge in illicit human magic has put the Church Enforcers on high alert. 
* The Shadow Informant: An unknown agent is systematically leaking the hidden existence and vulnerabilities of the supernatural underworld into human hands.
* Powder Keg: With Church enforcers cracking down and territorial rivalries already at an all-time high, Longred sits on the verge of an all-out war between the Holy Tribunal, rogue magic users, and the night-factions.
